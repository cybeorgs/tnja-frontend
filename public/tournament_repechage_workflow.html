1. Executive Overview

The TNJA Tournament Management System implements official International Judo Federation (IJF) bracket rules, offering both Single Repechage and Double Repechage draw algorithms alongside Round Robin pool systems.

Repechage Model Bronze Medals Awarded Who Enters Repechage Primary Use Case
Double Repechage 2 Bronze Medals (3rd Place) All 4 Quarterfinal Losers + 2 Semifinal Losers Official IJF Standard / World Circuit / Olympics
Single Repechage 1 Bronze Medal (3rd Place) Only players defeated by the 2 Finalists Regional / National Cup Championships
2. Double Repechage Match Flow (IJF Standard)

In Double Repechage, 2 Bronze Medals are awarded. Every athlete who reaches the Quarterfinals is guaranteed a chance to compete for a medal even if defeated.

Match-by-Match Execution Flow

  1. Quarterfinals (4 Matches): The 4 winners advance to the Semifinals. The 4 defeated players (QF 1, QF 2, QF 3, QF 4 losers) enter Repechage Round 1.
  2. Repechage Round 1 (2 Matches):
    • Rep Match 1: QF 1 Loser vs QF 2 Loser → Winner moves to Bronze Match 1.
    • Rep Match 2: QF 3 Loser vs QF 4 Loser → Winner moves to Bronze Match 2.
  3. Semifinals Crossover (2 Matches):
    • SF 1 Loser crosses over to Bronze Match 2.
    • SF 2 Loser crosses over to Bronze Match 1.
  4. Bronze Medal Finals (2 Matches):
    • Bronze Match 1: Winner Rep 1 vs SF 2 Loser → Winner gets 3rd Place 🥉
    • Bronze Match 2: Winner Rep 2 vs SF 1 Loser → Winner gets 3rd Place 🥉
  5. Gold Medal Final:
    • Winner SF 1 vs Winner SF 2 → Winner gets 1st Place 🥇, Loser gets 2nd Place 🥈.
3. Single Repechage Match Flow

In Single Repechage, 1 Bronze Medal is awarded. The system traces the path of the Finalists after the final match completes.

Match-by-Match Execution Flow

  1. Final Match Completion: Main bracket concludes to identify Champion (Gold) and Runner-up (Silver).
  2. Trace Finalist Competitors: System retrieves the specific athletes defeated by the Champion in the Quarterfinals & Semifinals.
  3. Repechage Match:
    • Champion's QF Defeated Player vs Champion's SF Defeated Player → Winner advances to Bronze Contest.
  4. Bronze Medal Final Match:
    • Repechage Winner vs Runner-Up's Defeated Contender → Winner receives 3rd Place 🥉.
